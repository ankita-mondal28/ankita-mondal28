## About

I'm Ankita — a second-year BCA student at Sister Nivedita University, Kolkata, working across the full stack (React, Vite, vanilla JS, MERN) and into AI/ML. I like building things that actually ship: real deployed apps over polished-looking demos.

My design taste runs editorial-glassmorphism, light-mode-can-be-premium-too, motion that means something — inspired by systems like Linear and Vision Pro spatial design. No gradient blobs, no dark-mode-by-default just because.

Longer term, I'm working toward deep AI/ML expertise through a Master's at a government college, via the CUET-PG / WB JECA route.
